# Workflow: Incident and Hotfix

**Trigger:** active production impact or urgent risk reduction. **Inputs:** impact, authority, affected boundary, rollback option, and critical journey.
**Procedure:** confirm impact and ownership; contain blast radius; preserve timestamps and evidence; classify risk; obtain immediate approval where required; apply the smallest reversible mitigation; verify the critical journey and rollback path. **Done when:** impact is reduced, mitigation and rollback evidence exist, and follow-up ownership is explicit.
**Risk and approval:** urgency never waives R3 approval. **Output artifact:** mitigation record with timeline, authority, checks, and residual risk.
**Terminal statuses:** use `../workflow-contracts.md`. **Verification evidence:** impact signal, critical journey, mitigation result, and rollback result.
**Recovery:** record follow-up as a separate task and preserve all incident evidence.
