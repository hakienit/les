# Workflow: Knowledge Promotion

**Trigger:** a task finding may be reusable project knowledge. **Inputs:** verified finding, source evidence, owner location, and update trigger.
**Procedure:** separate facts from inference; confirm durable scope and duplication; choose the project-owned location; write the minimum statement with owner and expiry/verification trigger; link the source evidence. **Done when:** durable knowledge is promoted or explicitly declined with reason.
**Risk and approval:** do not promote secrets, temporary state, private context, or unverified inference. **Output artifact:** knowledge record with owner, scope, evidence, and expiry.
**Terminal statuses:** use `../workflow-contracts.md`. **Verification evidence:** source task identity, checks, and promotion decision.
**Recovery:** keep uncertain material in the handoff, not durable knowledge.
